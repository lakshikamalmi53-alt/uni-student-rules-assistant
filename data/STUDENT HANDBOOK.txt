## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1. Horizon Campus
Founded on the principles of excellence, equality, and opportunity for all, Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt) Limited is recognized as one of the country's premier educational institutions. Established as a Private Limited Liability Company under the Companies Act on March 13, 2008, it holds Registration No. PV62877. Additionally, it was registered in 2008, under BOI law with Registration No. 3765/13/03/2008.

The "Horizon Campus", a trademark owned by Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt) Limited, was established in 2011 and is registered with the Intellectual Property Office of the Registrar of Companies.

Horizon College of Business and Technology (Pvt) Limited (Horizon Campus henceforth) has been recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Ministry of Higher Education (MoHE) for its local and affiliated programs. Furthermore, it is a member of the Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACU), the American Council on Education (ACE), the Asia Pacific Quality Network (APQN), and the International Network for Quality Assurance Agencies in Higher Education (INQAAHE).

Headed by its Founder Chairperson, Mr. Upul Daranagama who is a well-known personality in the field of education, Horizon Campus is strategically located in a picturesque location in Malabe, on the outskirts of the busy Colombo metropolis. The ultra-modern learning environments comprising state-of-the-art purpose-built infrastructure with all facilities such as modern lecture halls, laboratories, sports facilities and student recreation areas contribute to the holistic development of undergraduate and postgraduate students. These facilities are made available at two locations in Malabe, namely the Knowledge City Malabe and the HCBT complex along with the regional centre, which is located in the heart of Kandy city.

Horizon Campus (HCBT) has successfully launched several degree programs through its seven faculties: Law, Management, Information Technology, Education, Science, Technology and Engineering, and the Department of Nursing. Some programs have been independently developed by Horizon Campus to address the evolving needs of academia. However, striving for excellence in achieving its key objectives, Horizon Campus has collaborated with a portfolio of highly ranked local and international universities such as the University of London (UK), Birmingham City University (UK), Arkansas State University (US), Nilai University (Malaysia), Asia e University (Malaysia), La Trobe University (Australia) and University of Moratuwa (Sri Lanka).

### 1.2. Our Vision
> "To be the 'Centre of Excellence' in providing a truly inspired learning experience in a holistic environment"

### 1.3. Our Mission
> "To empower students by developing skills to excel academically, professionally, and as active members of society through a dynamic learning environment that promotes cutting-edge research and capacity building, to meet the ever-evolving global demands of the intelligent world"

### 1.4. Academic Partnerships
HORIZON CAMPUS has collaborated with International Universities, namely, The University of London UK, Nilai University-Malaysia, Birmingham City University- UK, Asia e University-Malaysia, La Trobe University-Australia, and locally collaborated with the University of Moratuwa to offer Bachelor of Information Technology Degree. Horizon CAMPUS-HCBT is also a recognized International Vocational Training Centre of Pearson Edexcel, UK's largest awarding body of Academic and General Vocational qualifications.

### 1.5. SLANSHEI - Founder Member
Horizon Campus is a founder member of the Sri Lanka Association of Non-State Higher Education Institutes (SLANSHEI). Established in 2016 through the initiation of seven leading private higher education institutes to address the needs of and provide leadership to the non-state higher education sector in the country.

## 4. ACADEMIC & STUDENT AFFAIRS

### 4.1. Registration of Students
Every student entering the university should register as a student of Horizon Campus and renew their registration annually. Re-issuance of a misplaced student identity card incurs a fine of **Rs. 1,500.00**.

### 4.2. General Structure and Evaluation of Degree Programmes
* **Semester System:** 8 semesters over 4 academic years. Minimum total credits = **120 credits**.
* **Credit Value:** 1 Credit = 15 hours of lectures OR 30 hours of practical work.
* **Attendance:** Minimum **80% attendance** is compulsory to sit for end-semester examinations.
* **Grading System & GPA:**

| Grade | Grade Point Value (GPV) |
| :---: | :---: |
| A+ | 4.00 |
| A | 4.00 |
| A- | 3.70 |
| B+ | 3.30 |
| B | 3.00 |
| B- | 2.70 |
| C+ | 2.30 |
| C | 2.00 |
| C-* | 1.70 (Minimum Pass Grade per module) |
| D+ | 1.30 |
| D | 1.00 |
| E | 0.00 |

> *Note: Overall FGPA must be a minimum of **2.00** to be awarded the degree.*

* **Award of Classes:**
  * **First Class:** FGPA ≥ 3.70 (Completed within 4 years)
  * **Second Class (Upper):** FGPA ≥ 3.30 (Completed within 4 years)
  * **Second Class (Lower):** FGPA ≥ 3.00 (Completed within 4 years)
  * **Pass:** Minimum FGPA ≥ 2.00

### 11.5. Borrowing Limits & Overdue Fines

#### Loan Allowances:
* **Undergraduates:** 2 Lending Books, 1 Reference Book
* **Postgraduates:** 2 Lending Books, 1 Reference Book
* **Academic Staff:** 4 Lending Books, 2 Reference Books

#### Fine Structure:
* **1st Week overdue:** Rs. 30.00 per day
* **2nd Week overdue:** Rs. 40.00 per day
* **3rd Week and onwards:** Rs. 50.00 per day
* **Scheduled Reference Books:** Rs. 100.00 per day

## 13. BY-LAWS & DISCIPLINARY MATTERS

### 13.1.9. Schedule of Offences and Punishments

| Offence | Recommended Maximum Punishment |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Refusal to produce Student ID** | Suspension up to 1 year / Severe Warning |
